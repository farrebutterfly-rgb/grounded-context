## 1 Purpose
This policy sets the rules for business travel and expense claims at Ekvall Torne Förvaltning AB.
## 2 Hotels
The hotel cost limit is 1 800 SEK per night including breakfast.
## 3 Per diem
The per diem for domestic travel is 290 SEK per full day.
## 4 Receipts
Expense claims with receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase.
## 5 Approval
Travel abroad must be approved in advance by the employee's manager.
