## 1 Parties
This agreement is made between Ekvall Torne Förvaltning AB (the Client) and Skärlunda Städ AB (the Contractor).
## 2 Scope
The Contractor cleans the Client's offices on floors 1 to 4 at Verkstadsgatan 9 every weekday. Window cleaning is not included.
## 3 Term
The agreement starts on 2026-02-01. Following amendment 1, the agreement ends on 2027-06-30.
## 4 Fees
Following amendment 1, the monthly fee is 46 500 SEK excluding VAT from 2026-07-01. The fee is invoiced in arrears on the first working day of each month.
## 5 Payment
Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date.
## 6 Termination
Either party may terminate the agreement with a notice period of 3 months.
## 7 Amendment history
Amendment 1 replaced sections 3 and 4 of version 1.
