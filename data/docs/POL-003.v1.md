## 1 Scope
This policy applies to all employees and contractors with access to company systems.
## 2 Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
## 3 Laptops
All laptops must use full disk encryption.
## 4 Incidents
Security incidents must be reported to the IT desk within 24 hours.
## 5 Review
This policy is reviewed every 12 months.
