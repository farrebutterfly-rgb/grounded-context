## 1 Background
Finance has reviewed travel costs for the first quarter of 2026.
## 2 Hotels
The hotel cost limit is 2 200 SEK per night including breakfast.
## 3 Receipts
Expense claims with receipts must be submitted within 45 days of the purchase.
## 4 Per diem
The per diem for domestic travel remains 290 SEK per full day.
