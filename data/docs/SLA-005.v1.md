## 1 Service
Fjällgren IT-support AB provides IT support for all employees of Ekvall Torne Förvaltning AB.
## 2 Response times
Priority 1 incidents receive a response within 2 hours. Priority 2 incidents receive a response within 8 hours.
## 3 Availability
The support portal has a monthly availability target of 99.5 percent.
## 4 Service credits
If the availability target is missed, the Client receives a service credit of 5 percent of the monthly support fee.
## 5 Support fee
The monthly support fee is 18 900 SEK excluding VAT.
