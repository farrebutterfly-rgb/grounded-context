## 1 Parties
This lease is made between Parkhus Väster Drift AB (the Landlord) and Ekvall Torne Förvaltning AB (the Tenant).
## 2 Spaces
The Tenant rents 24 parking spaces in the garage at Verkstadsgatan 11.
## 3 Rent
The rent is 1 150 SEK per parking space per month excluding VAT.
## 4 Term
The lease runs from 2026-03-01 until further notice, with a notice period of 6 months.
## 5 Deposit
The Tenant pays a deposit of 27 600 SEK before access cards are issued.
